LEGAL NOTICE
House Design Hub Philippines is an online marketplace and does not itself provide architectural or engineering services. Each plan is the work and responsibility of the licensed seller who lists it. Soil, wind and seismic conditions vary by site. The buyer should have the plans reviewed for their lot and local rules under the National Building Code (PD 1096) before construction. Each purchase allows the buyer to build one house from the plans; copying or reselling the files is not allowed.
